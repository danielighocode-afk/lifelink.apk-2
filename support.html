Quick answers

How does LifeLink charge?

LifeLink charges a flat fee, currently ₦100 to the customer and ₦100 to the provider for each booking. It is taken from your LifeLink wallet: held when a quote is accepted, released if the booking is cancelled early, and charged once the provider’s arrival is confirmed. The price of the job is agreed between you and the provider.

Is the money I pay for a job protected?

No. LifeLink does not hold or guarantee the job payment, and your wallet only covers LifeLink’s flat fee. Agree the scope and price in the message thread first, and do not pay for work until you are comfortable.

My wallet top-up did not show.

Wait a few minutes and check your wallet history. If it still has not appeared, email us the Paystack reference or a screenshot of the payment.

The provider did not arrive, or something went wrong with a job.

Open the booking and report the problem, or send us a message from the Trust & Safety page. Please give us the details and any photos so we can look into it.

How are providers verified?

A LifeLink administrator reviews each provider’s government-issued ID (such as a NIN) or trade certificate before they can send quotes. The Verified badge shows this review was done. It is not a guarantee of the quality or safety of any job.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile, then Privacy & Security, then Delete My Account (or use Settings). If your account has bookings, it cannot be deleted automatically because booking history must be kept, so email us and we will help.
