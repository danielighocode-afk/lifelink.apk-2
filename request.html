What happens next

  1. Verified providers see your request and send quotes.
  2. You compare quotes, message providers, and accept one.
  3. Your booking is created. You agree a time with the provider.
Requests and quotes are free. To confirm a booking, both you and the provider need at least ₦100 in your LifeLink wallet; it is only charged once the provider arrives and you confirm it. You pay the provider directly for the job.
