Trust & Safety

How to stay safe on LifeLink, and how to tell us when something is wrong.

What LifeLink does

  • Providers apply with an identity or trade document, and an administrator reviews it before they can send quotes.
  • Reviews can only be left by the customer of a completed booking.
  • Either side of a paid booking can open a dispute, which an administrator reviews.

What is not active yet

  • Escrow or payment protection. LifeLink does not hold the job payment, so agree payment terms carefully and never pay for a job before you are comfortable. Your wallet only covers LifeLink's flat fee.
  • Live map tracking and an AI assistant.

Staying safe

  • Agree the scope and price in writing before work starts. Use the message thread on your request so there is a record.
  • Never share passwords, one-time codes or bank PINs. LifeLink will never ask for them.
  • Be careful with anyone who asks you to pay or chat outside LifeLink.
  • In an emergency, contact local emergency services directly.
