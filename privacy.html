Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. Who we are

LifeLink (“LifeLink”, “we”, “us”) is an online marketplace in Benin City, Nigeria that connects customers with independent service providers through our website and mobile app. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. For any question, contact us at danielighocode@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, password (stored securely by our sign-in service), profile photo and banner, and your role (customer or provider). If you use Google sign-in, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Provider details: business name, services, prices, product listings, photos, and the documents you send us to be verified, such as a government-issued ID (for example a NIN) or a trade certificate.
  • Requests and bookings: service requests, quotes, job addresses, photos, chat messages, ratings and reviews, and reports or disputes.
  • Location: if you allow it, your location to find providers near you, and live location while a job is being tracked or arrival is confirmed. You can refuse or turn off location permission in your phone settings.
  • Payments: when you top up your wallet, payment is handled by Paystack. We do not see or store your full card details. We keep the amount, status, Paystack reference and your wallet history.
  • Device and notifications: a notification token for your device, the device type and app version, and basic technical logs that help us keep the service running.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account, and to connect customers with providers.
  • To verify providers and keep the community safe.
  • To process wallet deposits and LifeLink’s flat fees.
  • To send you notifications about your requests, quotes, bookings and messages.
  • To look into reports, disputes, fraud and misuse.
  • To fix problems, improve LifeLink, and meet our legal duties.

4. Who we share it with

  • The other person in a booking. Customers and providers see the details needed to do the job, such as names, photos, messages, the job address and, while tracking is on, live location.
  • Service providers that help us run LifeLink: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Paystack (payments), MapTiler (maps), Google (sign-in and Android notifications), your device’s push-notification service, and our website host. They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities when the law requires it, or to protect people’s safety.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Verification documents

Documents you send for verification are kept in private storage. Only LifeLink administrators can open them, and only to check your identity or trade. They are never shown to customers: customers only see a Verified badge. We keep them while your provider account is active and for a reasonable time afterwards for safety, fraud-prevention and legal reasons, and then delete them.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account from Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete My Account (or from Settings). Accounts that have bookings cannot be deleted automatically, because booking, payment and dispute records must be kept. In that case contact us and we will remove or anonymise everything we are allowed to. We may keep some records for as long as the law requires.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections, private storage for sensitive files, and access controls so people only see what they need. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong password and never share it or any one-time code. LifeLink will never ask you for your password, PIN or card details.

8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict some uses, to withdraw your consent, and to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. To use any of these rights, email danielighocode@gmail.com.

9. Children

LifeLink is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. If a change is important, we will tell you in the app. Questions? Email danielighocode@gmail.com or visit our Support page. Please also read our Terms of Service.
